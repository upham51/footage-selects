02Who it is for

03Specification

What it measures, reads and writes.

Scores
Focus, exposure and stability, from a few frames in every second of every clip.
Reads
Any mix of cameras, formats, sizes and frame rates, including phone footage, rotated clips and proxies.
Speed
Two hours of 4K is scored in about 15 minutes on an Apple silicon Mac. HD footage and proxies are usually quicker.
All-intra
ProRes and DNxHR unpack only the frames that get scored, several times faster than H.264 or HEVC of the same size.
Tuning
Sliders change the selects at once. Footage is never analyzed again when you move them.
Camera moves
Long selects are cut where the camera is still. A pan, push or drone reveal can stay whole.
Learns
Traces the shots you used in finished videos and fits the sliders toward your choices.
Exports
.xml for Premiere Pro and Resolve, .fcpxml for Final Cut Pro, an .edl and a .csv.
Privacy
No upload, no account. Analysis, selection and export make no network connections.
Platforms
Mac with Apple silicon or Intel, and 64-bit Windows.

How it works

  1. 04 · Step one

    Drop in a folder.

    Folders inside it are included. Several clips are scored at once, and selects appear as each one finishes.

  2. Step two

    Tune with sliders.

    Set how sharp, well exposed and steady a moment must be, and how long. A graph for every clip shows why.

  3. Step three

    Export the timeline.

    Each select sits above its source clip with a marker naming its strength and its three scores.

05Today and planned

What it does today, and what is planned.

The two lists are kept apart on purpose. Nothing under planned exists yet.

TodayThe base app, in early testing
  • Scores focus, exposure and camera stability for every clip, using classical image math rather than machine learning
  • Judges log footage in stops through the camera's own curve, reading the profile from Sony and DJI camera files
  • Finds the shots you used in videos you already finished, even graded, slowed down or letterboxed, and fits the sliders toward your choices
  • Cuts long selects where the camera is still and keeps a camera move whole
  • Reads mixed cameras, formats, resolutions and frame rates, including phone footage, rotated clips and proxies
  • Builds selects you tune with sliders, updated as you move them
  • Shows a graph for each clip with every kept and dropped stretch, and the reason
  • Exports timelines for DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro and Final Cut Pro, plus an edit list and a spreadsheet
  • Starts and ends selects in a pause when someone talks, and flags wind noise, distorted sound and dropouts
  • Flags crooked horizons, light flicker and drone jello, and white balance that differs from the rest of a scene
  • Works out golden hour, blue hour, daylight and night from when and where each clip was recorded, and groups clips into scenes
  • Lets you keep, drop and star any stretch by hand, and learns from those calls
  • Cuts a reel to the beat of a song you choose, in 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1
  • Saves a still of every select with a contact sheet, and writes a report on how the shoot went
  • Runs on Mac (Apple silicon or Intel) and on Windows, offline, free, with no account
PlannedOptional smart features
  • Spot near-duplicate shots and keep the best of each
  • Label shot types such as aerial, wide, detail and tracking, and search them in plain words
  • Arrange selects into a simple story order, with timecode order one click away
  • Learn your taste beyond the sliders, from what your finished videos used and left out
  • Keep a searchable library of everything you have shot

07Questions

Common questions.

Does the app upload my footage?

No. The base app never uploads footage. It reads your clips on your own computer and works without an internet connection.

What would the smart features send, and when?

Nothing unless you turn them on. Some planned smart features run entirely on your computer. The optional cloud features, when turned on, send only a few small still frames from moments already selected, never video files or your whole library, and the app will say exactly what is sent before anything leaves.

Is it free?

The base app is free. The smart features are planned as a paid option at a price still to be set. They are not available yet and nothing is charged today.

Does it use AI?

The base app does not. It measures sharpness, exposure and shake with classical image math. The planned smart features would add machine learning, mostly on your own computer, with an optional cloud part that is off unless you turn it on.

Which computers and editors does it work with?

Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) or Intel (macOS 11 or later), and 64-bit Windows. It exports timelines for DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro and Final Cut Pro, plus an edit decision list and a spreadsheet.

What does joining the waitlist commit me to?

Nothing. You will get an email when there is something to try. Ask to be removed at any time by replying to any email from us.
